Compliance & Safety Information

Data Residency: Customer data is processed and stored exclusively within the selected geographic region. NovaPulse does not transfer personal data across region boundaries without explicit customer consent.

Certifications: NovaPulse holds SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certifications, reviewed annually by an independent third-party auditor. Audit reports are available to Enterprise customers upon request under NDA.

GDPR & CCPA: NovaPulse acts as a data processor under GDPR. A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is available for all paid plans. California residents may exercise rights under CCPA by contacting privacy@novapulse.io.

Incident Response: In the event of a data breach, affected customers will be notified within 72 hours in accordance with GDPR Article 33 obligations.
